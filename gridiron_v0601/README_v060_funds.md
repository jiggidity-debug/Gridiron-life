# Gridiron Life v0.6 — starting funds adjustment

- Renamed the family-roll display from **Estimated household starting funds** to **Player starting funds**.
- Player cash is explicitly personal spending money, not total family wealth or savings.
- Added three rare affluent profiles to each parent pool of 75, with matching careers and annual incomes of $260,000, $540,000, or $980,000.
- Adjusted the starting cash calculation. Typical families still use income / 350 with a $35 floor; higher-income households (accessible income >= $200,000) use income / 250, capped at $3,000.
- Changes apply to **new careers**. Existing saved characters keep their money and family as generated.
