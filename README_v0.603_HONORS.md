# Gridiron Life v0.603 — College Honors & Showcases

Adds a persistent college honors archive, including conference and national weekly recognitions, First/Second Team All-Conference, divisional All-American, position and player-of-year awards, and first-year recognition. JUCO awards are tagged separately from the four-year divisions.

Honors are generated from actual saved player game statistics and minimum participation thresholds. National honors here mean performance benchmarks within a player's division, **not** an NPC ballot or a full national comparison. Honors are never invented for missing games.

A college postseason all-star/prospect showcase becomes available after qualifying seasons (including JUCO). The player can complete one simulated showcase per college year; its result is saved, affects scouting evaluation modestly, and adds fatigue. It is not a new on-field mini-game.

Existing v0.603 fixes, including week-by-week playable college/NFL offseasons and the stuck 29/30 activity-hours repair, are retained. Existing imported saves are supported, with retrospective recognition assessed from game logs when the College screen is opened.
