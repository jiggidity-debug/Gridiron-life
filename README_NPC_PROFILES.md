# v0.601 NPC Profiles & Academics

- HS teammates are now clickable in the compact Team position room, full roster, and legacy Team depth charts.
- Profiles are read-only and show attributes, relevant position skills, personality, academics, and development sessions. Untracked NPC technique badges/scheme mastery are not invented.
- Every newly generated HS NPC receives a persistent overall GPA. On each school week, that GPA moves slightly according to discipline, work ethic, and chance; past yearly GPAs are archived. Existing-save NPCs receive academics when first evaluated.
- Academic risk is displayed alongside NCAA core-course qualification caveats. **An overall GPA cannot alone certify NCAA eligibility**. This update does not simulate NCAA core courses or NPC scholarship offers.
- HS rosters presently simulate only the controlled player's position group; college and NFL NPC rosters remain on their older systems.
- The previous v0.601 freshman range and 90% optional-training rules are preserved.
