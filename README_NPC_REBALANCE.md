# v0.601 NPC starting ratings and development rebalance

- Newly generated ninth-grade NPC positional OVR is 20–45. Rating is calculated, not assigned for display only.
- Starting ability varies with youth experience and natural measurables; gifted players can have strong physical tools despite limited independent training.
- NPC discretionary-time patterns: 60% casual, 30% occasional, 9% dedicated, 1% football-obsessed. The 90% majority do not **regularly** use their free time to train.
- Team practice is available to all NPCs. Independent training is automatically estimated; it is not a separate player bonus.
- Weekly improvement is applied to a sampled position-related attribute with fractional progress and diminishing returns, not blanket annual +0–3 to each attribute.
- Some physical growth continues through maturity independent of football commitment.
- Older initial NPC cohorts are given modest simulated past development.
- Existing saved NPCs are **not rerolled**, but receive the new weekly development system going forward. Start a new career to see the fresh 20–45 cohort.
- NPC simulation currently covers the high-school positional roster; future college and NFL full NPC rosters will need their own career progression implementation.
